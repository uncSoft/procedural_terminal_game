# W10-story-flow: A2: the story column shows each turn from its start. Bookkeeping uses widget references, not child indices, and the continue screen is safe.

Read RULES.md and DECISIONS.md (same folder as this brief) first. The owner's decisions override the plan's approach wherever they differ.

## Approach (from the fix plan)
- Mount a zero-height marker at the start of each turn. _new_page removes everything before it, and _record_shown logs from it.
- _fixture_moment mounts a fresh sprite and freezes the old one in place (no move_child).
- Scrolling: after the turn's first piece, scroll_to_widget(marker, top=True). Follow streaming narration only when the bottom is already in view. A new floor page starts with scroll_home.
- Cap the room card at about 45% of the story's visible height.
- pick_up_again sets busy, records the seam rows before awaiting the recap, and logs mechanic rows with the live verdict word.
- The reroll line uses fate_left().
- Pick-ups get a compact '+ Potion → pack' line style.
- PageUp and PageDown scroll the story.
- Headless tests at 100x30 and 150x46 check that the '> command' line and the picture's top are visible after entering a room.

Files likely involved: src/ptg/ui/game_screen.py, src/ptg/ui/app.tcss, tests/test_ui.py, tests/test_continue.py
