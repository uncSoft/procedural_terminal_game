## 7. Progression: needs design first ❓ (JT: "don't jump into that")

What's there now, for the design to start from:

- **The campfire** has three tabs (Blessings, Gifts, Secrets) and the Hall of Legends.
  - **Blessings:** eight, each bought once. Vigor and Greater Vigor are the only chain.
  - **Gifts:** a choice of one starting item a run.
  - **Book of Secrets:** counts the hand-written moments found.
- **Souls** are spent only at the campfire, never in a run.
- **Perks:** fifteen, one picked from three at each level-up.
- **Traits:** nine, earned by how you play (talking monsters down makes you Silver Tongued...).
- **Weapons:** twelve. Only one has a power of its own (the Snitch: goblin bane); four are tagged holy or fire, which counts against weaknesses.
- **Coatings:** holy water or lamp oil poured on a blade makes it holy or fiery for a few hits. That's a temporary "fusion" already.
- **Already decided:** 10–12 floors over time, darker each level; an endgame reached over several runs; floor 5 opens after a first win.

The ideas, for a design pass (each wants its own plan, like docs/plans/multi-foe-fights.md):

- **Unlocks and achievements** (Binding of Isaac style): items that join the loot pool only once earned; an achievements table at the campfire beside the Book of Secrets. This is likely the backbone of the endgame over runs: collecting, a souls goal, floors opening.
- **Levelable blessings:** e.g. Vigor I–V, each +5 HP at a rising price, with a cap; the Blessings tab becomes a short tree.
- **The campfire / descend screen**, redone around those two.
- **Souls in the run:** a shrine or a character that takes souls for something now (a reroll, a blessing for this run only).
- **Classes from level-ups:** perks grouped into paths (a fighter's, a trickster's, a mage's) whose later picks lean on earlier ones, so a character becomes something.
- **Magic weapons:** weapons with powers that do things (a fire rod that burns, a frost blade that slows). Burning and stunning now exist, so these have something to hook into.
- **Fusion:** making coatings permanent ("lava bottle + cleaver = fire cleaver"): a fire-tagged consumable or trinket and a weapon make a weapon with that power, at a shrine or by Granny, so old gear stays useful.
