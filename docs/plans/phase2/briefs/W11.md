# W11-here-panel: A5/A4 UI: a persistent 'Here' grid for the room's items and characters, keeping the story column free of inline cards.

Read RULES.md and DECISIONS.md (same folder as this brief) first. The owner's decisions override the plan's approach wherever they differ.

## Approach (from the fix plan)
- New ui/here.py: a fixed 'Here' grid, placed where the owner chooses and fed by RoomView.
- One tile per character, item, feature and fixture, drawn with Picture at 24 px (12 px only when the grid is short) and animated.
- Calm NPCs appear in colour with their current line.
- Each tile opens its verbs (Take, Equip, Talk, Give…, Attack, Search, Use…) and sends engine actions directly.
- The grid rebuilds from RoomView every turn, so taken items vanish (no stale cards, no key matching). It lists every item.
- Remove _loot_moment, _show_loot and floor_cards. The story keeps one compact line per gain or exchange.
- Exchanges animate tile → pack or pack → NPC, labelled 'given to' / 'a gift from'.
- npc_say events render as exact-voice speech cards above the narration.
- Swoogy's guess becomes a 1-6 die picker with a new d6 reveal.
- On short screens, the grid replaces the sidebar room plan.

Files likely involved: src/ptg/ui/here.py (new), src/ptg/ui/game_screen.py, src/ptg/ui/widgets.py, src/ptg/ui/app.tcss, src/ptg/dice_art.py, tests/test_ui.py

### item-display
HOW ITEMS AND CHARACTERS ARE DRAWN TODAY (branch v2)

Core renderer, src/ptg/sprites.py
- Sprites are PNG strips in src/ptg/data/sprites. There are 98 of them, all 24 px wide: 54 item_*, 36 monsters/NPCs, and fixtures. Fixtures are 24x16, sunglasses 24x12, and nearly every strip has 2 frames.
- 51 hand-made 12x12 small versions live in sprites/small. Three items have none and get halved: lenny_s_bad_tooth, lenny_s_good_tooth, pizza_rat.
- Constants: DEFAULT_WIDTH=24 (:42), SMALL=12 (:43).
- _strip(key, small) (:71-85) loads a strip and cuts it into frames, cached.
- frames(key, width) (:56-68) uses the small strip if its width matches, otherwise _fit, which uses halve() at exactly half size and Image.NEAREST at any other size.
- has_small (:88) is used only by debug. frame_count (:121) counts frames at 24 px.
- sprite_text(key, frame, width, flash, pose) (:126) calls _render (:133, lru 512), then posed (:142, poses idle/windup/dizzy/calm/lunge/shake/death), then image_text (:159). The result is a half-block rich Text with no_wrap and overflow=crop. Those flags are lost when a caller append_text()s it into another Text.
- palette() and _nearest keep effect colours on the art's own palette.

UI helpers, src/ptg/ui/widgets.py
- portrait() (:164-170): a monster sprite at 24 px in a pose, or ASCII art.
- monster_text() (:184-203): portrait, HP bar, status line, optional 'weak to' line.
- AnimatedSprite (:279-334): a Static that updates sprite_text at `width` (default 24) on a set_interval. Methods: freeze / resume / become / play(steps).
- blank_art(role, width=12) (:463-475): grey stand-ins.
- item_picture(content, key, sprites, width=SMALL) (:478-487): sprite_text, then blank_art, then an ASCII glyph.
- item_tile() (:490-499): item_picture at SMALL plus a 2-line name and a note, in a 20x11 tile (app.tcss:553).
- room_text() (:132): the ASCII floor plan with item glyphs and a legend.
